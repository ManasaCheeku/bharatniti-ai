# BharatNiti AI — 3-5 Minute Hackathon Presentation Script

**Track**: Track 1 — AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance  
**Tagline**: *From Citizen Voices to Development Priorities*

---

## 0:00 - 0:45 | The Problem & Vision
*"Namaste judges! In a country of 1.4 billion people across 700+ districts, citizen feedback is heavily fragmented across multiple languages, local grievances, and disconnected channels. Policymakers struggle to know where to deploy infrastructure capital to make the biggest impact.*

*Introducing **BharatNiti AI** — an AI-powered intelligence platform that transforms fragmented citizen voices in regional languages into evidence-backed development priorities for government decision-makers."*

---

## 0:45 - 1:45 | The Citizen Experience
*"Let's demonstrate how a citizen in rural Mysuru submits a request in Kannada:*

`ನಮ್ಮ ಗ್ರಾಮದಲ್ಲಿ ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಇಲ್ಲ. ತುರ್ತು ಸಂದರ್ಭದಲ್ಲಿ 30 ಕಿಲೋಮೀಟರ್ ದೂರ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು.`

*Watch Gemini AI at work:
1. Automatically detects **Kannada**.
2. Translates to English context.
3. Classifies under **Healthcare → Primary Healthcare Access**.
4. Identifies an **Infrastructure Gap** score of 88 and an Urgency of 92.
5. Computes a transparent **Priority Score** of 91/100."*

---

## 1:45 - 2:45 | Policymaker Dashboard & Hotspots
*"Now switching to the Policymaker view:
- **KPI Summary**: Over 45+ seeded requests mapped across 10 Indian states.
- **Hotspot View**: Instantly reveals high-priority clusters like Mysuru (Healthcare: 91 priority score), Latur (Water Scarcity: 93), and Gaya (Education: 90).
- **Transparent Formula**: We show exact weights (Demand: 30%, Urgency: 25%, Infrastructure Gap: 20%, Affected Pop: 15%, Vulnerability: 10%)."*

---

## 2:45 - 3:30 | AI Policy Brief Studio
*"With a single click on **Generate AI Policy Recommendation**, Gemini synthesizes live district metrics and citizen demand into a comprehensive Policy Brief — detailing the problem summary, evidence base, recommended intervention (e.g. 24/7 PHC establishment), implementation steps, and expected impact."*

---

## 3:30 - 4:00 | Impact & Scalability
*"BharatNiti AI is ready for India-wide scale. Built on FastAPI, SQLite/PostgreSQL, React, Leaflet, and Google Gemini API (`google-genai`). It empowers digital public infrastructure to be truly citizen-centric.*

***BharatNiti AI doesn't just listen to citizens. It turns citizen voices into development intelligence. Thank you!***"
